# Amberlight

Amberlight turns an iPhone on your desk into a companion for your coding agents.
See at a glance which session is waiting for you, approve from your phone,
and know before you hit your limit.

**Amberlight Bridge** is the Mac side. It runs in the menu bar, reads your local
Claude Code and Codex sessions, and talks to the iPhone over your local network.
Nothing goes through our servers.
